# Demo video script — 2 minutes flat

Judges watch dozens of these. Problem first, product second, proof third.
Record screen at 1080p; narrate the voiceover **using Wispr Flow** (dictate it — that is
part of the story) and put "Voiceover: Wispr Flow" as a caption.

---

## Timed shot list

### 0:00–0:18 — The problem (hook)
- Screen: scroll a real (blurred) WhatsApp group full of forwarded assignment notices,
  a screenshots folder, random notes. Slight chaos is the point.
- **VO:** *"Assignments in college don't come in a calendar. They come like this — five
  groups, screenshots, 'Friday tak dena hai' — and the actual due date drowns."*
- Caption: **the problem**

### 0:18–0:35 — Introduce the tool
- Screen: clean Deadline Radar dashboard, slow scroll top → bottom.
- **VO:** *"This is Deadline Radar. Paste, speak, or photograph any of it — it comes back
  as one list that answers: what is due today, and what is getting late."*
- Caption: **Deadline Radar — link in description**

### 0:35–1:10 — Capture demo (the core)
1. Paste a **multi-line notice** (3 deadlines) → Enter → *"Captured 3 deadlines from one
   paste ✓"* — all three cards land in the radar. **(8s)**
2. **Auto-detect beat:** copy a message in WhatsApp (desktop), switch to Deadline Radar →
   the 📋 "Clipboard check" card pops **by itself** → tap **✓ Add**. **(8s)**
   *(Android bonus: installed app → Share → Deadline Radar captures via the PWA share target.)*
3. Click 📷 → pick a notice screenshot → "Reading…" → task extracted from the image.
   **(9s)**
4. Hold Wispr key, speak one hands-free → text lands → Enter. **(9s)**
- **VO:** *"One paste — and it even catches what I copy from WhatsApp on its own. Photo, or
  straight from my mouth — same radar."*
- *(Alt if a take goes wrong: paste a single messy message `dbms ka assignment friday
  tak, 20 marks…` → one structured card with date + marks.)*

### 1:10–1:40 — What it gives back
- Screen: the **"🎯 Ab kya karun?"** decision card at the top (start a ⏱ focus with
  one click), then the **🕐 Aaj ka timetable** — today as a bell schedule (time ranges,
  late task opens the day; ▶ starts a real focus block), the
  **radar dial** (rings fill by urgency, sweep rotating, on-time %
  in the centre), the **🎯 marks-at-risk** line, and the **🌦️ 7-day weather forecast** —
  tap the ⛈️ storm day to filter to it. Every card now carries a **🎯 risk dial**
  (0–100, inked by deadline proximity + marks + your own lateness history) — hover one
  so its reasons show as a tooltip. A card shows its **🧠 prediction chip**
  ("Usually 2.0d late — start NOW"), the **🔥 12-week consistency heatmap** glows in the
  rail, and the **📈 6-week finish arc** shows the on-time/late split *over time*.
  Search, sort and filters now sit on the **🗄️ DeskBar** pinned right above the cards.
  Tick a task done — **confetti bursts** — tick the
  **🔁 weekly** lab record and next week's deadline **rolls itself onto the radar** —
  then tick the last pending task: the **whole radar is clear** and confetti falls
  from every edge. Open **Weekly report** — a **🏅 letter grade (A+…D /100)** is
  rubber-stamped on the top of it.
- **The plan moment (new):** click the **📋 list button** on any card → **"Plan banao"**
  lays the deadline out as **day-by-day study sessions** — what to do in each round,
  on which day, ~how long, ending in "submit" — press **Copy plan** and the checklist
  lands in the chat. **(8s)**
- **Voice beats:** press **🔊 Briefing** — the radar *speaks* your whole day
  (late → today → tomorrow → on-time rate → "ab kya karun" pick). Then press
  **🎙️ Ask** and say *"ab kya karun?"* → the radar
  **speaks the pick** ("Start now: SE slides… about 1.3h") + on-screen card. **(10s)**
- **Then order it, don't just ask it:** press **🎙️ Ask** again and say *"mark DBMS done"*
  → the **card ticks itself** with a spoken "Done: DBMS worksheet" (+3s). Say
  *"delete OS lab"* and it's gone with an undo flash. One button — questions *and* orders.
  Quiet-time beats if the take is calm: toggle **☑️ Bulk select** and clear three tasks
  in one click; show a **📅 GCal** chip landing a deadline straight into Google Calendar. (+6s)
- **VO:** *"The dial is the whole picture at a glance — red means trouble, green centre is
  your on-time score. It counts marks at risk, learns my own rhythm from history — and it
  doesn't just warn me, it plans me: my whole day is time-blocked, one click gives any
  deadline a short day-by-day checklist, and the weekly report is stamped with a real
  grade, not vibes."*
- Tap a dial ring → it jumps to that section. Show the **On time** ring, the **📈 6-week arc**
  and Weekly report numbers — this is the "one real result".

### 1:40–1:52 — The voice story (challenge twist)
- Screen: Wispr Insights usage card glimpse + a clip of coding with voice (terminal/AI
  tool visible, no hands typing).
- **VO:** *"The whole build was voice — spec, code, docs — with Wispr Flow. That's my
  usage card: [X] words, zero typing."*
- Caption: **#NoHandsChallenge**

### 1:52–2:00 — Close
- Screen: live link + repo on screen.
- **VO:** *"Deadline Radar — built hands-free. Try it: [link]."*

## Production notes

- **Tool:** OBS (free) or Clipchamp for screen + cut. Keep one take per section, cut tight.
- **Voiceover:** dictate every line with Wispr Flow, then paste the transcript where you
  need to trim — double win: narration + usage numbers.
- **Blur** real names/messages in the problem shot; replace with sample data elsewhere.
- **Length check:** hard stop at 1:59. If over, cut the voiceover story section first,
  never the capture demo.
- **End frame:** link + "Built hands-free with Wispr Flow".
- Export 1080p, upload before 30 Oct evening.
