# 🎙️ Daily dictate routine (2 min/day)

Wispr usage card ke liye: **roz ek script bol do** — that's it. Scripts by weekday rotate
hote hain (weekly cycle). Kahi bhi bolo — Notepad, the app's own input box, anywhere.
Hold your Wispr key → speak → done.

> Ye sirf usage ke liye nahi: har script tumhara **real progress journal** hai — weekly
> check-in post, demo VO, aur submission write-up isi se banega.

---

## Monday — Build standup (~100 words)

"Aaj ka standup. Maine Deadline Radar pe capture flow pe kaam kiya. Pehle tha ki paste karne
pe date aur marks extract ho jaate the, ab photo se bhi task ban jaata hai. Do problems aayi:
ek thi ki emoji render nahi ho raha tha empty state me, aur doosri thi og image ka text card ke
peeche chhup jaata tha. Dono fix ho gaye. Kal ka plan hai weekly report ko better banana aur
demo script ko finalise karna. Wispr ke saath hi sab dictate kar raha hoon, to usage bhi ban
raha hai."

## Tuesday — Feature brainstorm dump (~95 words)

"Aaj ke liye feature ideas. Ek to recurring deadlines — har hafte wale assignments automatic
repeat ho. Doosra syllabus import — pura syllabus daalo aur sab deadlines radar pe aa jaayein.
Teesra shared radar — hostel group ke saath, ek banda capture kare sabko dikhe. Chautha exam
week mode — exams wale hafte me assignments ka pressure alag se dikhao. Yeh sab voice me dump
kar raha hoon taaki baad me backlog bana saku aur submission me ideas mention kar sakoon."

## Wednesday — Wispr formatting practice (~90 words)

"Aaj formatting commands practice. New line. Bullet point: pehla point capture by voice.
Second point capture by photo. Third point daily reminder. Caps DBMS. Question kya offline
parser Hindi dates samajh sakta hai? Answer abhi nahi, isliye AI wala option hai. Full stop.
Yeh practice zaroori hai kyunki demo video me bhi sab voice se banana hai, aur formatting
commands se typing zero rehti hai."

## Thursday — User story (~95 words)

"Ek user story likhta hoon. Main ek second year ka student hoon. Meri DBMS assignment ka
message group me aata hai aur main ignore kar deta hoon kyunki date kahin aur likhi hai.
Friday ko pata chalta hai ki deadline kal hai. Deadline Radar me main wo message paste karta
hoon aur app turant batata hai: due kal hai, twenty marks hai, high priority hai. Ek daily
notification bhi milta hai. Result: main deadline se ek din pehle submit kar deta hoon.
Aur on-time counter badhta hai."

## Friday — Demo VO rehearsal (~90 words)

"Aaj demo video ki voiceover practise. Assignments in college don't come in a calendar. They
come like this: five groups, screenshots, friday tak dena hai. This is Deadline Radar. Paste,
speak, or photograph any of it. It comes back as one list that answers: what is due today and
what is getting late. The whole build was voice: spec, code, docs, even this video. That's
my usage card. Deadline Radar, built hands-free. Try it."

## Saturday — Weekly review (~95 words)

"Hafte ka review. Deadlines captured kitni, kitni done, kitni on time — yeh numbers app ke
weekly report se. Wispr usage is hafte dictation kitni hui. Kya sab voice se hua ya typing
karni padi. Rukawatein: ek billing ka renewal date dekhna tha, doosri image extraction ki
accuracy. Next week ka focus: demo video ka final cut aur submission form ki fill up.
Yeh review bol ke main weekly check-in post ka draft bhi bana leta hoon."

## Sunday — Check-in post dictate (~85 words)

"#NoHandsChallenge ka check-in post. Day seven of the no hands challenge. Built Deadline
Radar, a voice first deadline tracker for college. Paste, speak or photograph any assignment
and it extracts the task, subject, deadline and marks into one urgency sorted radar.
Everything this week was dictated, not typed. One month of Flow Pro is on us. Link in the
description. At WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow. Hashtag no hands challenge, hashtag Wispr Flow."
