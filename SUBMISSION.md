## 5. "A few lines" — paste-ready write-up

> **Problem.** In college, deadlines arrive as random WhatsApp messages, screenshots,
> syllabus lines and voice notes — nothing lives in one place, and the actual due date
> drowns when the same notice is forwarded across five groups. Deadline Radar captures any
> of it (paste, speak, or a photo of the notice) and pulls out the task, subject, deadline
> and marks, then answers one question every morning: what is due today, and what is
> getting late?
>
> **Who it's for.** Students first, any stream — the exact person whose group chat is their
> project management tool. The same capture flow works for anyone who receives deadlines in
> chat instead of a calendar: interns, junior employees, freelancers.
>
> **How I used Wispr Flow.** Every word of this build was dictated, not typed — the product
> spec, the requirements and follow-ups fed to my AI coding tools, the docs, the README and
> this write-up. The product itself is voice-native: hold a key and speak an assignment, and
> it lands as a structured task — and it can even answer "aaj kya hai?" out loud. Wispr
> usage: [___ words dictated — from your usage card].

> [__] of them finished **before** the deadline — the app measures this itself in its
